# Support

Need help with a Magic Fox game? Email **[magic.fox.game@gmail.com](mailto:magic.fox.game@gmail.com)**.

Please include the game name, device model, Android or browser version, game version, and a short description of the problem. For purchase issues, you may include the Google Play order identifier, but never send passwords or bank-card details.

## Pixel Color Quest

- Progress is stored locally on the device.
- Purchases are handled through Google Play.
- Clearing app data or uninstalling the game may remove local progress.

See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) and [Terms of Use]({{ '/terms/' | relative_url }}).
